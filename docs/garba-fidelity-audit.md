## Link reconciliation (colourStory ↔ linked products ↔ image)

The audit exposed a root cause: three of the six failures had product links that *contradicted* the look's own colour story, and the generator followed the products, not the story.

| Look | Problem | Fix applied |
|---|---|---|
| CL-G-03 | her footwear was a black jutti inside a "navy + silver" look | → **VRS-W-0341** Mirror Sequin Work Kolhapuris (Oxidised Silver) |
| CL-G-07 | her main was a **Multicolour** brocade chaniya inside a "white + multicolour mirrors" look (hence the coloured render) | → **VRS-W-0029** Patola chaniya choli in White |
| CL-G-14 | his entire side was ivory while the colour story said navy + ivory | → **VRS-M-0038** Bandhani Tonal Embroidery modern ethnic shirt in Navy (duplicate ivory stole dropped) |
| CL-G-19 | his main was an **Emerald** kurta (hence the green render) | → **VRS-M-0019** Kutchi Motif Embroidery garba look in Ivory |
